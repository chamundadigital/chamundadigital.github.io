Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Clear, Fair & Transparent Terms

1. Nature of Digital Goods

At ChamundaDigital, we provide intangible, digital products comprising downloadable AutoCAD .DWG architectural plans, 3D Elevation renders, Vastu design collections, and engineering bonuses.

Because these digital products are delivered instantly via cloud links and cannot be physically "returned" or "un-downloaded" once access is granted, all sales are generally considered complete once full digital delivery has taken place. However, to ensure 100% customer satisfaction and fair business practice, we have established clear guidelines below under which cancellations and refunds are granted.

2. Eligible Refund Conditions

We gladly issue a full 100% refund in any of the following circumstances:

Duplicate / Accidental Double Payment

If your bank account or UPI was debited twice for the same transaction due to a network glitch or page refresh, we will refund the duplicate amount immediately.

Access / Technical Delivery Failure

If you completed payment but were unable to access the files, and our support team is unable to deliver your valid download link within 48 hours of reporting.

Corrupted or Unreadable Files

If files are proven to be corrupted or damaged, and our technical support is unable to replace or restore them for you.

Payment Deducted, Order Incomplete

If payment was successfully debited by the gateway but the order was dropped without receipt generation, full refund is guaranteed.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds cannot be processed under the following scenarios:

  • Change of mind after successfully receiving access and downloading files to your system or cloud storage.
  • Lack of suitable software (such as AutoCAD, DWG viewers, or PDF software) where system requirements were already clearly specified on the landing page prior to purchase.
  • Requests made beyond the 7-day refund request window.

4. Refund Processing Time & Mode

Processing Turnaround: 5 to 7 Working Days

Once your refund claim is verified and approved by our support team, the refund is initiated through our payment gateway partner.

The amount will be credited back directly to your original source payment method (same Bank Account, UPI ID, Debit/Credit Card, or Wallet used during the transaction) within 5 to 7 business days as per standard banking settlement rules.
